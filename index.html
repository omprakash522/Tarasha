Ring Size Guide

1 Paper Strip Method

Cut a thin strip of paper or use a thread. Wrap it around the finger you plan to wear the ring on. Mark where the paper overlaps with a pen.

2 Measure the Length

Flatten the strip and measure the length in millimeters using a ruler. This is your finger's circumference.

3 Find Your Size

Match your circumference to the table below. If between two sizes, choose the larger one for comfort.
